## Summary

The party headed through the forests east of [[City of Rillstead|Rillstead]], fought off a [[House Drachenweld|Drachenweld]]-led bandit ambush, looted their camp, and discovered a (likely) Quickling-killed body bearing an Owl Crest. They arrived at an impromptu garrison of the [[Blackmere Wardens]], where [[Sir Falkwin]] revealed his men are under attack from bandits, traps, and mysterious nightly "jinxes". The party has agreed to investigate, suspecting [[House Drachenweld|Drachenweld]] manipulation of local Fey.

---

## Key events

### Rillstead East Road Bandit Ambush

Fought a bandit group led by a [[Factions/Ranks/Drachenweld Scout|Drachenweld Scout]] firing distinctive black barbed arrows. Assisted by Lion's Mane Mercenaries. Interrogated survivor Jerry, looted the bandit camp. Bodies buried.

### The Quickling Body

Found an unrecognisable corpse on the road with Sylvan text carved repeating "Thief, Killer, Murderer..." — believed killed by Quicklings.

### Meeting Sir Falkwin of the Blackmere Wardens

Arrived at a garrison camp bearing the same Owl Crest as the dead body. Met [[Sir Falkwin]] and the Blackmere Warden's Cleric [[Oswald]] (follower of [[Agathys - Goddess of Fate]], Goddess of Fate). Falkwin's men are being harassed by bandits, man traps, and nightly shadow attacks — men believe it's a curse, Falkwin suspects witchcraft. Crescentia offered to help after Falkwin produced a corpse riddled with the same black barbed arrows from the earlier ambush.

### The Party's Theory

The party believes the Drachenweld have either tricked local Fey into targeting the Blackmere Wardens, or the Fey have simply misidentified who is responsible for murders and thefts. Party has agreed to investigate for Falkwin.
